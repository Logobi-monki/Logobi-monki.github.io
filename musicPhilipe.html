A simple framework for understanding a text and its message

SOAPS

Speaker

The speaker is the person or voice communicating the message. Consider their point of view, background, and credibility, and how these may shape the way the text is written.

Speaker image placeholder

Occasion

The occasion is the context that prompted the text. It can include the time and place, important events, or circumstances surrounding its creation.

Occasion image placeholder

Audience

The audience is the person or group the text is intended to reach. Consider what the audience may already know, value, or need, and how the speaker addresses them.

Audience image placeholder

Purpose

The purpose is what the speaker hopes to accomplish. A text may inform, persuade, explain, entertain, or encourage its audience to take action.

Purpose image placeholder

Subject

The subject is the main topic of the text. Summarize the central idea and note the important points the speaker uses to develop it.
